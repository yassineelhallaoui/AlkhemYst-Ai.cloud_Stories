# Experiment Provenance Record: Reproducing and challenging: whole blood DNA extraction methods and Infinium 450K DNA methylation (Hjorthaug et al. 2018, Oslo University Hospital and University of Oslo)

*Assembled from the provenance record, which was written as each run ended rather than reconstructed afterwards.*

## What happened, in short

This pipeline was run 12 times on 2026-08-19. 12 of those runs completed.

They covered 7 distinct configurations: some were the same canvas run again, which is repetition rather than a new experiment.

The pipeline was renamed during this period. It was called "Reproducing and challenging: whole blood DNA extraction methods and Infinium 450K DNA methylation (Hjorthaug et al. 2018, Oslo University Hospital and Universit" before it became "Reproducing and challenging: whole blood DNA extraction methods and Infinium 450K DNA methylation (Hjorthaug et al. 2018, Oslo University Hospital and University of Oslo)". Each entry below carries the name it had at the time.


7 of those were tests and 5 were kept as experiments. That distinction is the shape of the work: it says which results were stood behind at the time and which were still being poked at.

All of this work was done by YassY Elhallaoui.


No run has been deployed, so this record describes work in progress rather than a result that has been put to use.
